How TaxEase calculated it

Important

TaxEase provides simplified estimates only. Actual tax depends on the transaction, taxpayer status, exemptions, deductions, credits, and current BIR rules.

Made easier for beginners:

TaxEase never asks you to guess a tax percentage. It only calculates the supported Philippine tax situations shown in the form. If the selected case needs a different rule, TaxEase stops and explains the limitation instead of giving a misleading result.
